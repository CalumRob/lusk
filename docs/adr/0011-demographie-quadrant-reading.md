# The Démographie story reads the quadrant of two annualized rates — not growth vs the région baseline

The Démographie story ("Attractive ou fertile ?", `docs/themes/demographie.md` §Story pool) shipped with a 2×2 classifier locked 2026-08-03: *relatively growing/shrinking* (net growth rate vs the **région median**) × *natural/migration dominant* (|solde naturel| vs |solde migratoire|) → fertile / attractive / vieillissante / exode. The first real-data run exposed the flaw: the growth axis was **relative to the région median**, so a territory with both forces positive (net arrivals *and* natural renewal — the healthiest shape there is) could still be read `exode` because its growth sat below the Breton average. Concretely, commune 22004 (solde naturel −228, solde migratoire +309 — net growth +81 driven by migration) was classified `exode`. The reading contradicted the territory's own numbers, and the chart — raw counts, axes auto-fitted from 0 — could not show why. The "bug" was not the classifier's arithmetic; it was the axis it measured against.

**Decision (user, 2026-08-04):** the Démographie reading is re-derived from the **signs of the two soldes expressed as annualized rates per 1000 inhabitants** — the four quadrants of a plot whose axes cross at 0. The région-median axis is dropped; nothing relative enters the reading. Concretely:

- **Rates (pipeline-computed, INSEE convention):** `taux_solde_naturel = (naissances − décès) / 6 / population_moyenne × 1000` and `taux_solde_migratoire = solde_migratoire / 6 / population_moyenne × 1000`, where the period is the inter-censal span (2017→2023) and `population_moyenne = (POP 2017 + POP 2023) / 2`. The raw soldes stay in the payload; the rates are added columns.
- **The four readings are the four quadrants** (positive means `> 0`; exactly-zero joins the negative side): **attire-renouvelle** (both positive — the population renews and attracts), **attire-meurt** (migration positive, natural negative — arrivals mask a dying population), **vide-meurt** (both negative — the population empties and dies), **vide-renouvelle** (natural positive, migration negative — births mask an exodus). The old names (fertile/attractive/vieillissante/exode) are dropped — they encoded dominance distinctions that no longer exist.
- **Story renamed:** "Attractive ou fertile ?" → **"Trajectoire démographique"**. The one-liner stays a fixed per-quadrant sentence; the "comment lire" line quotes the territory's actual rates (a step toward issue #74's "stories source their data").
- **The chart confronts the territory with its own context** (issue #75, absorbed here): the quadrant frame with axes at 0, no median lines; a background cloud of the territory's comparison group **at the same scale** — a commune sees its EPCI's communes (or, when it belongs to no EPCI — two exist in Bretagne — its département's communes); an EPCI sees the other EPCIs of the region; a département the other départements; the région all its communes. Every cloud point is hoverable (its name + rates) and **clickable — it opens that point's own fiche**. The région now shows the story too (its dot among all communes), the classification always published. *(Scope corrected 2026-08-05: the original draft said "a commune → its EPCI's communes; an EPCI → its communes; a département → its communes"; the agreed reading is comparison-at-same-scale per type, and the story sources itself exhaustively — the two datasets it reads (série historique for the rates, base des EPCI for the groups) cited from the vintages table, issue #74 seam.)*

**Considered options:** (1) keep the locked 2×2 and fix only the chart — rejected: the quadrant and the label would disagree for below-median-growth communes, relocating the confusion instead of removing it; (2) keep a dominance tie-breaker inside the quadrants (TR splits "fertile" vs "attractive") — rejected: the old names forced a lie on a territory that is both; (3) **re-derive the reading from the rate-quadrant — chosen**.

**Consequences:** the payload `histoires` schema gains `taux_solde_naturel` / `taux_solde_migratoire` (R side + TS mirror + fixtures, `docs/architecture.md` §The fiche payload); the R classifier (`compute_histoires_demographie`) is rewritten — deterministic, sign-only, testable with the 4-commune fixture (mixed-sign cases like 22004 now have a correct home in `attire-meurt`); the app maps the four new classification keys to copy (`storyDemographie.ts`); the chart (`GraphiqueSoldes.vue`) is rebuilt as a quadrant with a clickable context cloud; the story block sources itself exhaustively from the vintages table (published as `vintages.json`, issue #74 seam); `CONTEXT.md` gains the "Trajectoire démographique" term and the Solde entries now name the rates; `docs/themes/demographie.md` §The 2×2 is replaced. The régional median that anchored the old growth axis is gone from the story — the rank-in-context indicators (`rang_reg` etc.) still provide relative positioning where the fiche wants it; the story itself now speaks only the territory's own two forces.
