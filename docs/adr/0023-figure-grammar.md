# Les figures de la fiche suivent une grammaire fermée — huit familles, une coquille de carte, une règle de compacité

The fiche's figures were **un-grammared**: every theme rendered its reading as a **full-width ~280 px slab**, one hybrid card rendered every indicator regardless of its nature, and a year of locked-but-unexecuted presentation decisions (Notes.md #196, #199, #200, #201, #202, Partie II items 35–44, Partie III 62–65) was never applied. Each chart was bespoke — adding a style meant touching every theme — and the page read as stats, not report: the reading figure dominated the prose instead of sitting beside it. The fiche follow-up (#367) locked the decisions; this ADR records the grammar they form.

**Decision (user, 2026-08-12):** the fiche's figures become a **closed grammar of eight preset families** — scalar, composition, trajectory, distribution, relationship/cloud, list, pyramid, comparison bars — each rendered by the **one shared figure renderer that switches on the family** (never a per-theme branch). A subgroup declares its figure's family and indicator in the theme metadata (`theme_<theme>.json`); the app renders what the payload declares (ADR-0020), it never chooses. Every grid figure shares the **card shell**: the **payload-owned label** (never hardcoded, no raw key ever renders), the **rank chip « Xᵉ / Y »** with the **direction glyph** — ▲ « plus = mieux » / ▼ « moins = mieux », the full sentence always in tooltip and aria-label (**the glyph never carries meaning alone**, color-blind safe, DESIGN.md §2/§8) — and the **vintage stamp**. The **scalar accent** is a quiet **theme-ramp edge accent keyed on rank position** (strong top third, faint middle third, none bottom third) — **direction-safe by construction** because the rank is already direction-corrected (« 1er est toujours bon », ADR-0015): it reads *position*, never *judgment*, and it is **never a status color**. The **compactness rule**: **no figure exceeds ~200 px tall**, reading figures sit **compact beside the prose**, **only the prose is full-width**. **distribution_dpe renders in the official A→G colours, never the theme's palette** — the one sanctioned palette override, so the fiche speaks the same visual language as the DPE label on the door.

**Considered options:** (1) keep the full-width slabs — rejected: the chart dominated the page, the fiche read as stats not report (US 9); (2) per-theme figure components (16 bespoke renderers) — rejected: drift, and adding a style means touching every theme (US 16: one shared renderer, one module per family); (3) an open chart library with no family list — rejected: the eight families are a consistency promise ("styles prédéfinis des figures"), the family is payload-declared and validator-restricted; (4) status colors on the accent (success/warning semantics) — rejected: color-blind unsafe and judgmental — the rank is already direction-corrected, the accent only says where the territory sits; (5) keeping the DPE on the theme palette — rejected: the DPE label on the door is official A→G, the fiche must speak that language (US 7).

**Consequences:** the app implements **one shared figure renderer** switching on the family (the app wave, #371); the theme descriptors declare family + indicator per subgroup (the metadata wave, #370); the reading values (div_loss, tot_loss, soldes rates, quadrant forces, top-5 LQ) become **indicators of their subgroup** rendered in their family's figure style — the **clouds replace the chip**, the div_loss distribution keeps chips on the numbers; a subgroup may declare **no reading** (the honest silent state, first-class). The compactness rule **retires the full-width slab**. The grammar is documented in **DESIGN.md §9** and the ordinality per indicator in **Méthodes** (« Le sens des classements », ADR-0015's direction table); CONTEXT.md gains the reworked-indicator terms. The DPE carve-out is the **one sanctioned palette override** — recorded so a future palette tightening never silently re-colors the DPE. The carte mirrors the new decomposition per ADR-0019. This ADR meets the record bar: the grammar is **hard to reverse** (payload contract + shared renderer + validators), **surprising without context** (the accent-as-position rule, the glyph-never-alone rule, the 200 px cap, the DPE carve-out), and **trade-off-driven** (compactness trades chart fidelity for reading pace; the accent trades color semantics for glanceability; the glyph trades glyph-for-meaning against accessibility, mitigated by the mandatory sentence).
