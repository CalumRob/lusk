# La Page d'indicateur devient l'artefact canonique data-first - les cartes sont des vues de l'indicateur, la Carte épargnée reste routée mais sans lien

Two artifacts now share the product, and only one of them was ever written down. The **fiche d'identité** is territory-first: it gathers every indicator for ONE territory across six theme tabs. The **Page d'indicateur** is data-first: one canonical page follows ONE indicator across all comparable territories (`/indicateurs/:theme/:indicator`). The pages shipped through a long wave (#409, #437–#441, #468, #472–#474) without an ADR recording the architecture they form, while three decisions underneath them stayed formally owned by older records: ADR-0007 (the fiche opens on an **Aperçu** default tab), ADR-0019 (**la Carte, miroir de la fiche**, a standalone destination and the product's entry point), and the provisional wording left in `CONTEXT.md` by the header prototype (#400). The atomic cutover #410 settled all three on tested production behavior; this ADR records what shipped.

**Decision (user/PO, 2026-08-26):**

- **The Page d'indicateur is the canonical data-first artifact.** Themes group pages in the catalogue (`/indicateurs`) but have **no pages of their own** — themes and subgroups are titled rassemblements, never destinations. Shipped coverage: **34 published pages across the six themes** (Mobilité 16 · Habitat 7 · Démographie 4 · Économie/Emploi 3 · Milieux 2 · Programmes et subventions 2), each at `/indicateurs/:theme/:indicator` with three stable views — **Repères** (default synthesis: distribution, contrasts, extremes, direction-aware ex-aequo ranks), **Carte**, **L'indicateur**. Pages cover indicators only; **lectures stay fiche-side** until a data-first editorial model for them exists, but every indicator remains page-eligible even when a lecture interprets it.
- **Maps are views of an identified indicator, never a destination.** Each page's **Carte view** (`?vue=carte`) maps exactly the one scalar comparison facet resolved from the descriptor — the same facet that drives Repères' median, extremes and table ordering — within the same URL-explicit scope. The map does not have its own layer catalogue to browse: arriving at a map means having arrived at an indicator.
- **⚠️ The standalone Carte is SPARED — routed but unlinked (PO ruling, 2026-08-26).** This supersedes ADR-0019's standalone-destination status **without deleting it**: `/carte` stays registered and fully functional as the PO's personal exploration tool (its miroir-de-la-fiche content rule survives intact), but NO user-facing surface links to it — not the header, footer, home, search, lists, or reading figures. The entry-point role and the « context-giver » framing die with the cutover to the **Territoires · Indicateurs · Sources · À propos** header; the home's two equal doors are **Territoires** (the territories-only search) and **Indicateurs** (« Explorer les indicateurs » → the catalogue).
- **Programmes et subventions replaces Aperçu as the sixth theme, first and default on the fiche** (#408). The identity anchors (population, densité, superficie, composition) are gone entirely — prototype verdict #400: **Remove**, nothing persists in a fiche header. This supersedes ADR-0007's Aperçu-default decision; the payload-driven tab-bar principle it introduced survives unchanged (the bar renders exactly the payload's themes, in canonical order — now six, opening on Programmes et subventions).
- **The published theme metadata is the single runtime authority.** The descriptor `theme_<theme>.json` owns catalogue membership (`indicator_pages` — planned-but-unpublished documentation creates neither a page nor a dead link), each page's family and comparison facet, labels, units, direction treatment, supported levels, caveats, and its `source_records` provenance. The validators restrict every field; the app renders what is declared, it never chooses. Méthodes being retired (#410 — `/methodologie` deleted, no alias), dataset provenance renders once from those same `source_records`: the **Sources** page for datasets with consumers, embedded source cards + a « Voir la fiche » anchor on each page's **L'indicateur** view.
- **Handoff state contract (PO-approved Option A, 2026-08-26):** a fiche's « Explorer » passarelle carries **its territoire + its niveau when comparable** (commune/EPCI/département; the Région is excluded from data-first comparison, so its handoff carries the territoire WITHOUT niveau and the page resolves its honest fallback) plus the theme/indicator via route params. **The facet is never carried**: the target page resolves ITS canonical facet from the descriptor. The carried territory stays highlighted across Repères and Carte until cleared or replaced; the level follows the URL-explicit chain (explicit URL → visitor's remembered level when supported → finest published level, written back into the URL).

**Considered options:** (1) keep the standalone carte as a browsable destination alongside per-indicator maps — rejected: two map entries compete and the layer-browsing model hides the indicator behind a lens choice; the PO ruling keeps the tool alive precisely so this remains a judgment call for exploration, not a product path; (2) delete `/carte` outright — rejected by the ruling: the tool works and the PO uses it; deletion would also orphan the `apercu` table that still feeds its popup; (3) give themes their own landing pages between catalogue and indicator — rejected: an empty intermediate hop, the catalogue's themed groups already do that work; (4) let the handoff carry the fiche's active detail/sexe into the page — rejected (Option A): the fiche tab and the page facet answer different questions; carrying the facet would couple two navigations that evolve separately, while the descriptor-declared facet guarantees every visitor lands on the page's canonical reading; (5) keep Aperçu as a non-theme rundown with Programmes anchored inside — reversed by #400/#408: the anchors duplicated theme facts, and Programmes earns more as a full theme tab than as an anchor slab.

**Consequences:** the product's IA is two parallel semantic families — territory-first fiches and data-first Pages d'indicateur — sharing the figure/facet vocabulary (ADR-0023's families, the Facette résumée, the direction-aware rank of ADR-0015/0021) but never their renderers: the fiche explains one territory, Repères compares many. ADR-0007 and ADR-0019 remain in force as history, marked superseded in part above; `CONTEXT.md` carries the retired statuses (Aperçu, Méthodes, Carte spared-but-unlinked). Future indicator pages require only a descriptor entry — catalogue, route, views, sources and search follow — which is the record's test: if wiring a new page needs app code, this decision has drifted.
