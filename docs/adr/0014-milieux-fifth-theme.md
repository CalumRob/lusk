# Milieux — the fifth theme block: the environment earns a block on the land axis first, the water axis after research

**Amended by [ADR-0017](0017-milieux-ocs-ge-pivot.md) (2026-08-08, issue #236):** the story's land force pivots from CONSOENAF *flows* to **OCS-GE states**. Superseded in the decision below: the flow-based reading of « Se densifier, s'étaler, ou s'en aller » (the story now reads Δpopulation sign × per-capita-state-trajectory sign); the « zéro consommation » test (dropped — the consumption-sign axis was degenerate, 99.3 % one-sided); the « renaturation potentielle, jamais mesurée » claim (now measured — M3 state < M2 state is real désartificialisation); and the **two-clocks** rule (extended to **three clocks**: population · OCS-GE state · CONSOENAF annual). ADR-0017 also locks the **bracketing population rule** (RP 2017 for the initial state, RP 2023 for the final state — never interpolation) and the **millésime-mixing rule** (per-département M2→M3 pairs, stated). The story's terms now live in `CONTEXT.md` (« état artificialisé », « trajectoire par habitant », « millésime OCS-GE », « trois horloges »).

Lusk shipped four themes (Mobilité, Démographie, Habitat, Économie/Emploi) with environmental concerns woven across them (DPE in Habitat, cycling in Mobilité, green economy in Économie), and the status of a dedicated environment/transition block stayed open since grilling 2026-08-04. A brainstorm about ZAN (2026-08-06) — why a green city like Rennes struggles with the zero-artificialisation objective — produced a primary-source research note (`docs/research/zan-rennes.md`) and the decision to add a **fifth theme, « Milieux »**: the state of the territory's natural milieux, on two axes — **land** (v1) and **water** (phased).

**Decision (user, 2026-08-06):** « Milieux » joins the fiche as a fifth theme block. **Land axis v1** ships two indicators — « Consommation d'ENAF » (ha over the post-loi window 2021-2025 + the annual series since 2011, ranked on the *part de la surface consommée*, never raw ha — big communes have more land) and « Trajectoire ZAN » (Δpace 2021-25 vs the 2011-21 reference decade, rank as-is); the destination mix (habitat/activité/routière…) is deferred. **The water axis is designed but its contract is deliberately NOT locked** — it is built only after a water-data research pass (the chain: commune ← UDI ← captage ← bassin d'alimentation, because a commune's water quality is decided upstream, often in other communes), exactly as the land side got before any design. The block's **thesis — environmental problems do not respect communal borders** (land leaks out of constrained centres into the ring; water arrives from elsewhere) — is what justifies a fifth tab. The **Tension** principle is its spine. The story is **« Se densifier, s'étaler, ou s'en aller »**, single-story, four readings by sign (threshold = 0: ZAN is a zero-objective and CONSOENAF is a complete census — a 0 is a real 0), intensity (m² ENAF per added inhabitant) shown only when Δpopulation is meaningfully positive, chart = the same-scale comparison cloud (ADR-0011). Two product-level rules were locked with the block: the **two-clocks transparency promise** (the indicator runs on the annual CONSOENAF clock and is *deliberately fresher* than the story, which is pinned to the population clock — its window derives from the RP millésimes the Démographie série historique holds, 2017→2023 today, and slides when RP updates, the land window re-summed to match; both stamped, Méthodes documents the gap) and the **population-sourcing rule** (population is never taken from CONSOENAF's embedded fields — census millésimes 2011/2016/2022 — always from the Démographie série historique, so one population truth per fiche).

**Considered options:** (1) keep the environment woven across themes, no block — rejected: the land axis is the one environmental dimension with policy teeth (a legal trajectory, annual rankable communal data, Licence Ouverte); a woven thread cannot carry its own story, Méthodes page, or the two-clocks honesty machinery; (2) block names — « Environnement »/« Écologie » too broad, « Sols » too narrow (cannot carry the water axis); « Milieux » chosen, the DCE vocabulary ("la qualité des milieux"); (3) water axis in v1 — rejected: the chain has open data questions (UDI→commune mapping, captage→UDI mix, BAC coverage for captages prioritaires only) that need their own research pass; shipping both axes risks a half-built axis and a Méthodes page full of "to be verified"; (4) a leakage story on the fiche (AAV ring reading) — rejected: the AAV is a statistical perimeter, not a policy lever, and the EPCI fiche's own cloud already shows the intra-EPCI centre-ring gradient; the AAV-level finding (RM −16 %, ring +7 %, AAV −2 %) stays in the research note as documentation, never on a fiche; (5) distribution-fitted thresholds for "consomme vs ménage" — rejected: ZAN is a zero-objective; (6) population for the intensity from CONSOENAF's embedded fields — rejected: two differently-vintaged populations on one fiche.

**Consequences:** the pipeline gains a Milieux theme descriptor — CONSOENAF manifest, the **m²→ha unit conversion** (the Cerema dictionary says hectares, the 2011-2025 file ships m² — verified via `artcom1125`; documented in Méthodes, never silently trusted), the trajectory computation from the native windows, and the two-clocks window logic (the story window derived from the série's millésimes, never hardcoded); the payload gains the two indicators, the `histoires` classification with the four reading keys, and the supporting intensity figure; `CONTEXT.md` gains the Milieux / Tension / story terms and resolves the open environment-block question (environment now owns a block *and* is woven across themes); the never-built "land-use in Habitat" thread moves to Milieux at zero migration cost (Habitat keeps its built-stock and market indicators — mix, statut/ancienneté, prix, DPE); the fiche gains a fifth tab; Méthodes gains a Milieux section (sources, the unit quirk, the two-clocks gap). The water axis is a v2 project gated on its own research — the chain concept is in CONTEXT.md, its contract deliberately not.
