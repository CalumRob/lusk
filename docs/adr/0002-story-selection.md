# Per-entity Story selection — one Story per theme, chosen by salience

The fiche contract ("4 indicators + one signature story per theme") broke down under its own weight: a single fixed story per theme is either universal-but-bland ("le littoral qui dort" for an inland commune says nothing) or interesting-but-conditional (the coastal story is meaningless for Rennes' hinterland). **Decision (user, 2026-08-03):** every theme carries a **pool of 1–3 candidate Stories**, and the pipeline selects **exactly one per fiche, per territory, deterministically** — the Story whose salience says the most about that territory. A Story must be an original computation (never a downloaded figure); a theme with a single candidate is legitimate and that Story *is* its default. Selection is a contract: same territory + same data → same Story, forever, documented in Méthodes and locked by the fixture pipeline tests.

**Considered options:** (1) one fixed story per theme — rejected: not universal, and "littoral qui dort" was nice but false for most of Brittany; (2) every indicator potentially a story — rejected: unbounded design surface, guardrail-violating (the story becomes the biggest number, not a computation); (3) no story for unremarkable territories — rejected: breaks "every fiche ends with a story"; (4) curated pool + deterministic salience selection — **chosen**.

**Mechanics (locked with the decision):** pool of **1–3 candidates per theme** (keeps themes grounded in simple indicators). One candidate is always-on as the **default**: the most universal computation — the one that applies to every territory, naturally the mildest of the pool. The others replace it only when their salience fires. Salience favours **computed combinations** (RS × distance to coast, vacancy × population trend) over raw outliers — the guardrail "innovation in service of the story, never in place of the data" survives. **Basse/absent salience → the default shows**, never a stretch. Names and exact salience thresholds are provisional per theme (see `docs/data-source-map.md`); the *model* is locked.

**Consequences:** the fiche payload's `story` field becomes selection output, not a constant per theme; the pipeline gains a deterministic story-selection step (testable with the 4-commune fixture); `CONTEXT.md`'s "Story" term carries the pool/salience/default semantics; the theme blocks in `docs/data-source-map.md` now list pools instead of single stories; a future reader looking at per-commune story variance should read this ADR, not "fix" the variance. The story model does **not** touch the 4-indicator standard block or the cross-theme "Programmes & financements" strip.
