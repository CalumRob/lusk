# La Carte, miroir de la fiche — the map's contents and scales derive from the payload, never a carte spec

The carte (`/carte`) shipped as a hardcoded single-indicator choropleth browser: `configCouche.ts` mapped two of five themes to one indicator each (Démographie → `densite`, Habitat → `part_passoires`), Mobilité/Économie/Milieux rendered empty masks, and the fixed ladders in `seuils.ts` flattened real distributions (Rennes at 4 582 hab/km² shared a 300+ bucket with 135 communes — a 15× range). The map's purpose was also undefined: it drifted between a finder and a mini analytical tool. A contents re-spec (grilled 2026-08-10) decided what the map *is* and what it *shows*, and the answer to both is "the fiche."

**Decision (user, 2026-08-10):** the carte is a **context-giver** — the fiche tells the story, the map puts it in context — and its content follows the **miroir de la fiche** rule: *every number a fiche renders is a layer on the map*. The layer list derives from the payload (the `indicateurs_<theme>.json` keys **and** the `histoires_<theme>.json` story scalars), never from a carte-specific spec. `configCouche.ts` dies. Consequences:

- **Default layer per theme** = the Story's first scalar in contract order (the scalars the fiche's story actually renders — α rule): Démographie → `taux_solde_naturel`, Mobilité → `div_loss_t`, Habitat → `part_passoires`, Milieux → the first artif scalar. Story-pool siblings (`div_loss_b`, `delta`…) and multi-detail keys become **grouped sidebar layers**. Series (conso_enaf, prix_m2 vintages) and distribution signatures (`dens_*`/`dec_*`) are excluded — a choropleth needs one value per territory.
- **Scale rules are computed from the values, never specified per indicator** (`SEUILS_INDICATEURS` dies): **signed** (`min < 0 && max > 0`) → diverging ramp — theme anchor at the positive pole, one shared counter-hue (coral) at the negative pole, neutral at zero, per-side tail-test buckets; **heavy tail** (`p99/median > 10`) → log-spaced sequential; **tame** → linear/quantile sequential.
- **The Aperçu tab is reversed (item 53 of Partie II):** the carte's first tab is renamed **« Programmes et subventions »** (named « Programmes & financements » in this decision; terminology aligned to the official dataset wording 2026-08-11, #356) — membership categorical highlight layers + subventions choropleth, **level-native** (ACV/ORT/PVD at commune, CRTE/ORT/Territoires d'industrie at EPCI, subventions total at every level, nothing invented at dép/région). Aperçu stays a fiche-only concept.
- **First load is the neutral state** (uncoloured masks, `?theme=` absent); **level switch re-joins the same layer** at the new level's rows; the **popup leads with the active layer's value + rank**, then « Voir la fiche »; the **search zooms to the entity and opens its popup**; the **hover renders below an open tooltip**, EPCI names wrap. Mobile map is deferred (a non-goal until desktop stabilises).
- **URL state:** `?theme=` (a theme) and `?onglet=programmes` (+ optional `&programme=` sigle) are mutually exclusive.

**Considered options:** (1) a carte-specific spec document listing which variables map — rejected: it drifts from the fiche, the whole point is that the fiche *is* the contract; (2) a payload table declaring continuous-vs-discrete per indicator — rejected: the scale rule is computed from the values, no table needed; (3) per-theme default judgment (pick the "interesting" scalar per theme) — rejected for a uniform deterministic rule (first story scalar in contract order); (4) continuous ramps for heavy tails — rejected: discrete log-spaced buckets keep the legible legend, log spacing fixes the flattening; (5) single-hue sequential ramps for signed variables — rejected: the sign is the point of ADR-0011, a diverging ramp is required; (6) keep Aperçu as a null state — reversed: it now has real content (programmes).

**Consequences:** the app's carte machinery is rebuilt around payload-derivation: `configCouche.ts` and `SEUILS_INDICATEURS` are deleted, the layer list and scales compute from `indicateurs`/`histoires` rows; the design debt of DESIGN.md §8 (single-hue choropleth ramps) is retired for the signed case; the fiche's `NOMS_INDICATEURS` becomes the label source for map layers (Habitat's empty label map is fixed by #200); Économie has no story scalar until its specialization-score ticket lands (the carte waits for it); **#207 is re-scoped** (its "remove the Aperçu tab" item is now reversed). CONTEXT.md gains the **Carte** entry and the Aperçu/Programmes entries are corrected.
