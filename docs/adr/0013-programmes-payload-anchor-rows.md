# Programmes & financements payload: anchor-level membership rows, ladder derived in the app

The fiche's **Programmes & financements** element (CONTEXT.md §Programmes & financements, ADR-0007) gained its full scope on 2026-08-06: three kinds of coverage — **labels** (ACV, PVD — commune-anchored closed lists), **contracts** (CRTE, Territoires d'industrie — EPCI-anchored), and the **ORT tool-badge** (badged only where it adds information) — plus **subvention facts** (level-dependent display: by-policy-area split on commune fiches, single annual total on EPCI/département/région fiches). The ladder has two voices: EPCI-anchored contracts **cover** their member communes (downward), commune-anchored labels reflect up as **named portage** (the EPCI fiche names its labelled member communes). The app already carries the seam: a typed `programmes` prop (currently defaulting to empty) in `ApercuOnglet.vue`.

**Decision (user, 2026-08-06):** the pipeline publishes **anchor-level membership rows only** — ACV/PVD **commune** rows, CRTE/TI **EPCI** rows, ORT **commune + EPCI** rows — plus **precomputed subvention aggregates**. The app derives the ladder (coverage-down and named-portage-up) from the existing `territoires.epci` reference, the same ladder the context switcher already joins. Subvention facts are pipeline-computed aggregates (the raw 101k-row SCDL data never ships — "the app renders, the pipeline computes"); the by-area split and the EPCI totals are numbers the pipeline owns. Every badge row carries its source's vintage stamp (ACV/PVD/CRTE/TI = dataset last-update; ORT = per-row "Dernière actualisation"; subventions = weekly).

**Considered options:** (1) **precomputed full table** — the pipeline publishes every territoire's final badge set with portage rows already expanded — rejected: redundant (a CRTE badge would appear on the EPCI row *and* every member commune row), a heavier contract to keep in sync, and the pipeline would re-implement the ladder it already publishes in `territoires.epci`; (2) **anchor rows + app-side derivation — chosen**: each badge lives exactly once at its anchor, the pipeline stays a faithful mirror of the official sources (data.gouv ANCT datasets + the DGALN ORT file + the SCDL subventions), and the derivation is a deterministic join.

**Why this does not violate the "never derived in the app" principle** (CONTEXT.md §Aperçu, ADR-0007): that principle guards **computed stats** — the `apercu` figures and rank-in-context numbers must come from the pipeline. The ladder derivation is a **relational join**, the same operation the app already performs for the context switcher. Joins are the app's job; computations are the pipeline's.

**Consequences:** `programmes.json` gains two tables — membership rows (territoire id, programme sigle, anchor level, source vintage) and subvention aggregates (commune-level **full per-area splits** — amended 2026-08-10, issue #305: the original top-N + « autres » guardrail is **removed** from the pipeline; the fold is a display decision, so the app owns top-5 + click-reveal, descending sort, the compact `X,XX M€` format, the context-share line, and the provenance line — see CONTEXT.md §Programmes & financements; only 78/725 communes had more than 6 areas, all exactly 7, so the payload grows trivially — EPCI+ annual totals). The app gains a derivation step over the existing ladder for coverage and portage; badge vocabulary stays in the app (`Programme { sigle, nom }`, `libelleProgramme`, `LIEN_SUBVENTIONS`); the pipeline owns sourcing the five datasets and aggregating the subventions. A future reader questioning why the payload is not "fully expanded" should read this ADR, not "fix" the payload.
