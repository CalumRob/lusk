# The Mobilité flagship design: a div_loss Story on a snapshot, an isolation-logic grid, and a supply sub-block

The Mobilité contract (`docs/themes/mobilite.md`) was locked 2026-08-03 before the ported analysis's real shape was in view: it promised a fixed-4 indicator block (accessibilité t/b/c, équipements BPE 229 types, voitures/ménage, réseaux OSM) and a single "flagship accessibility deep-dive" Story. Two contract revisions since then made it stale on its own axes: the fiche moved to a **variable** number of indicators per theme (2026-08-04, replacing the fixed-4 contract), and the **Méthodes contract** (2026-08-06) made a theme's documentation a shipping deliverable. The existing analysis in `E:\Website` (per-building accessibility, 1.2 M residential buildings, R5 routing, 20-minute cap, satiated utility over 53 selected BPE equipment types clustered into alimentation/santé/administration/école/banque, aggregated to commune/EPCI/département) carries far more than the old contract assumed — a dozen candidate metrics including `norm_score`, `div_loss`, `tot_loss`, `pct_iso`, deciles and density signatures, computed and production-verified.

**Decision (user, 2026-08-06):** the Mobilité flagship is redesigned around three decisions that shape the whole theme:

1. **The Story is `div_loss_t` — « Vingt minutes sans voiture ».** The story *is* the theme's headline: *"Sans voiture, X types de services disparaissent"* — the count of different service types that leave the territory's reach à pied ou en transports en commun at 20 minutes. A *count*, never an index: `norm_score` (the old log-normalized 0–100 vulnerability composite) is **rejected as too convoluted** — it hides its computation, a count does not. The Story renders the territory's building-level distribution (the pipeline's density signature, median marked) against a **same-scale comparison cloud** (ADR-0011's Démographie pattern: a commune vs its EPCI's communes, an EPCI vs the other EPCIs, a département vs the other départements, the région vs all its communes). The bike delta (`div_loss_t − div_loss_b`) is a **salience-fired second candidate — « Ce que le vélo préserve »** — firing only where cycling rescues real diversity (top quartile ~4+ types, top decile ~10; the median commune rescues ~1 — no story there). It always reads **realized** cycling access, never potential ("ce que le vélo permet déjà", not "ce que de meilleures infrastructures apporteraient" — the data shows the current network, not a counterfactual). The **région gets no special Story** — `div_loss_t` is well-defined at every scale and serves the région fiche as-is.

2. **The grid uses isolation logic, not access logic.** The five standard indicators are the **shares of buildings *without* access** per service cluster — `1 − share_*`, the mirror of the access share (the two are the same fact in loss vs access framing, never two indicators): alimentation, santé, administration, école, banque, all at 20 minutes **à pied ou en transports en commun**, each with rank-in-context. The deprivation framing is deliberate — a territory acts on what its buildings *lack*. The **Équipements BPE 229-types indicator is dropped**: the flagship already *is* BPE used as a destination layer; a raw equipment count says "what's here", not "what you can reach". `pct_iso_*` (car-dependence share) is **Story material**, not grid — `pct_iso_full_t` ("part des bâtiments qui perdent *tout* accès") has no grid mirror and carries the starkest reading.

3. **The flagship is a snapshot, and says so.** The analysis is still data on a slow clock (BPE annual, networks rarely, heavy compute) — not alive like the light themes. The fiche stamps it distinctly: "Analyse calculée le [date] — se rafraîchit sur un rythme lent", never mimicking the weekly-refresh vintage chips; the Story's « comment lire » quotes the snapshot date; Méthodes documents the slow clock as a first-class fact. **Vocabulary:** indicator labels say "à pied ou en transports en commun", never "sans voiture" (the phrase overclaims the bike, which is a separate, better mode) — the story *title* is the one sanctioned exception, its « comment lire » carrying the precision.

**The block.** Grid (5 isolation shares) + demand/network tier (Voitures par ménage RP · Réseaux t/b/c OSM) + **« L'offre de mobilité alternative »** sub-block (Offre TC Korrigo · Bornes de recharge IRVE · Stationnement vélo / 1 000 hab from the Ecolab hub, id `67f989c8d9b3a8440f204aa7` — the standing 2026-08-04 decision to take the hub figure as-is stands, since bike-parking counts are not derivable from Lusk's own pipeline). ODbL discipline (ADR-0001) extends to OSM · Korrigo · stationnement vélo.

**Considered options:** (1) keep `norm_score` as the headline — rejected, convoluted composite; (2) total loss (`tot_loss`, the satiated volume) as the Story — rejected, an opaque weighted figure where the count is legible; (3) bike as the Story's mode — rejected, everyone has feet; the bike is the *delta* story; (4) access-framed grid (`share_*`) — rejected, deprivation framing is what a territory acts on; (5) region-wide comparison cloud — rejected, flattens rural contrast; same-scale peers keep the local bite; (6) rebuild Équipements BPE or admin `pct_iso` — rejected, redundant or not-in-production; (7) treat the flagship like the light themes' freshness — rejected, it is not alive and must not pretend to be.

**Consequences:** `docs/themes/mobilite.md` is rewritten to this contract; `docs/data-source-map.md` §Mobilité is updated (5 isolation shares + sub-block trio, BPE dropped, Korrigo/IRVE/stationnement vélo promoted from 🔶 to locked); `CONTEXT.md` gains the terms (Perte de diversité, Part des bâtiments isolés, Vingt minutes sans voiture, Ce que le vélo préserve, L'offre de mobilité alternative, the Alive snapshot clause). The ported pipeline must apply `ensure_mode_neutrality` on the base before any loss aggregation (bike ≥ transit, always — the non-production summarize artifact showed negative deltas; the production file is clean). The pipeline publishes: the 5 isolation shares, the Story's `div_loss_t` + distribution signature + same-scale cloud points, the salience classification and its trigger value, and the block's five supply/demand figures — plus the snapshot date as a first-class vintage fact. The app maps two new Story keys (`vingt-minutes-sans-voiture`, `ce-que-le-velo-preserve`) and renders the isolation grid + sub-block; Méthodes documents the slow clock and the ODbL sources per the 2026-08-06 contract.

## Amendment (user, 2026-08-31, issue #530)

The new semantic Mobilité contract uses the **direct access shares** as its
canonical facts: all 15 `share_*_{t,b,c}` values (five services × three modes)
are published, typed, ranked high-is-good, and consumed by the new
`TerritoryFacts` / `ThemeContent` seam. The five `iso_*` rows remain only as a
temporary compatibility mirror for the legacy prototype renderer and will be
removed after #531 migrates that renderer. `tot_loss_t` and `tot_loss_b` remain
separate volume-loss facts. The Région keeps its own distribution signature but
does not receive a commune-comparison cloud; a regional cloud would be
misleading rather than useful.
