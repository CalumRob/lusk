For each member, contract class is the exact membership in `scalar_contracts`, `profile_contracts`, `owned_series_routes`, `observed_collection_contracts`, and `distribution_contracts`; source is `sources[ID]`. Mobility legacy exclusion is the finite explicit ID set shown in the table below, matching the proposal's excluded snapshot/share surfaces. AEDAR measures are separately enumerated from the producer formula documented below. Readers are evidence references, not traffic claims.

Validation on the pinned source: 6 metadata files; 62 theme-scoped indicator entries; 62 unique theme/ID pairs; among those indicator entries, 29 excluded, 1 unresolved, and 32 proposed candidates. Every source `(theme, indicator_key)` pair was compared with the table pair set; duplicate, missing, extra, or unclassified members fail the check. Contract-membership counts are 30 scalar, 9 profile, 4 owned-series, 2 observed-collection, and 1 distribution declarations; overlaps are intentional (for example `prix_m2` has scalar and owned-series declarations). Each class count was independently recomputed from its source map.

`source_records` additionally enumerates source identities per theme; the row's `source_id` is looked up by exact indicator ID in `sources`, not guessed from a name. The three special cases proved outside those five metadata maps are enumerated below from publisher declarations: `structure_age` profile, `raccordement_reference` named-reference alias, and `subventions_annuelles` owner series.
