AEDAR measures are separately enumerated from the producer formula documented below. Readers are evidence references, not traffic claims.

Validation on the pinned source: 6 metadata files; 62 theme-scoped indicator entries; 62 unique theme/ID pairs; among those entries, 29 excluded, 0 unresolved, 1 explicitly owner-retained, and 32 proposed candidates (33 retained-or-proposed). Every source `(theme, indicator_key)` pair was compared with the table pair set; duplicate, missing, extra, or unclassified members fail the check. Contract-membership counts are 30 scalar, 9 profile, 4 owned-series, 2 observed-collection, and 1 distribution declarations; overlaps are intentional (for example `prix_m2` has scalar and owned-series declarations). Each class count was independently recomputed from its source map. The 32 proposed candidates are not individually owner-approved.

`source_records` additionally enumerates source identities per theme; the primary `source_id` is looked up by exact indicator ID in `sources`, not guessed from a name. Declared input-source lists are preserved separately (the B316 ratio uses both `bpe_b316` and `bornes-recharges`). The three special cases proved outside those five metadata maps are enumerated below from publisher declarations: `structure_age` profile, `raccordement_reference` named-reference alias, and `subventions_annuelles` owner series. No unaccounted source-declared member is silently promoted. For the owner-retained B316 ratio, the source record currently disagrees with the authoritative Mobility input manifest (metadata says BPE 2024; manifest/reader pin BPE25/2025). This is a provenance-descriptor consistency correction for implementation, not a product-scope uncertainty. No runtime package, R startup, database, or source-data scan was used.
